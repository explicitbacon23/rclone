Privacy Policy

Application: rclone Personal Drive

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Overview

rclone Personal Drive is a personal-use application that allows the account owner to access and manage their Google Drive files from a Linux computer using rclone.

Google Drive Data Access

The application requests permission to access Google Drive files to perform operations initiated by the account owner, including viewing, uploading, downloading, modifying, and deleting files.

Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and OAuth tokens are stored locally in the account owner's rclone configuration. Files may also be cached locally to support filesystem operations.

Data Sharing

This personal application does not sell Google Drive data or intentionally share it with third parties. Data is accessed through Google's APIs and processed by rclone for the account owner's requested operations.

Data Retention and Revocation

The account owner controls locally stored files and cached data. Google Drive access can be revoked through the Google account's security settings.

Contact

This application is maintained for personal use. For questions, contact the application developer through the contact information associated with the OAuth application.
